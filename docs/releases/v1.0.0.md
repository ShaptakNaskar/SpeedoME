# SpeedoME 1.0.0

- Explore 38 map worlds, each with an original speedometer and matching colours and lettering throughout the app.
- Switch themes and worlds with arrows, or hold the name, slide onto a choice and release; the picker also scrolls at the edges in landscape.
- Record trip sections without interrupting the full recording, then save them linked to their trip or separately.
- Find settings in organized groups, with controls suited to the selected theme.
- Read clearer labels and buttons across light and dark themes, including Sunlight.
- See complete long-trip routes when zoomed out, smoother map panning and a clearer speed graph.
- Read trip time alongside speed, distance, maximum speed and averages.
- Keep your trips on your device, export GPX, and use the app without an account or analytics.

## Downloads

- **SpeedoME-1.0.0.apk:** signed universal Android release build, Android 8.0+.
- **SpeedoME-1.0.0-unsigned.ipa:** iOS 16.0+; requires signing with your own certificate before installation. The [iOS guide](https://github.com/ShaptakNaskar/SpeedoME/blob/v1.0.0/docs/ios.md) explains installation and platform differences.
