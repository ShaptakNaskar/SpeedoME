# Store listing (draft)

**Title:** SpeedoME: GPS Speedometer

**Short description (80):** Honest GPS speed with eight gorgeous gauges, trip stats, targets and a nerd page.

**Full description:**

SpeedoME shows the speed your GPS chip actually measures. It uses the receiver's own Doppler speed, cleaned by a filter that throws away impossible jumps, so you get no 200 km/h glitches in a tunnel and no lag when you pull away.

**Eight themes.** Swipe between them:

- Retro: a chrome-bezel 1970s gauge.
- Modern: a glowing arc.
- Digital: VFD seven-segment.
- Night Focus: only the speeds you need stay lit.
- Map: your route coloured by speed.
- Nerd: satellites, signal bars and G-force.
- Speed Tape: aircraft style.
- Sunlight: high contrast for bright days.

**A dial that fits.** Auto-range grows the scale as you speed up and shrinks it back afterwards. You can also pick a fixed dial.

**Speed limit.** Set a limit and every dial runs to 25 % over it, with the top in red. The needle and digits turn red as you get close, and the phone buzzes when you reach it, then pulses faster the further over you go.

**Trip computer.** Moving and overall averages, max speed, distance and time. Set a target distance to get an arrival estimate, or an arrive-by time to see how fast you need to go and whether you're ahead or behind.

**Walk & run mode.** Steps, cadence and pace. Your steps keep speed sensible when GPS is weak.

**Trips that survive.** Record a trip and it's saved every second. If Android closes the app, the trip resumes. Browse your history, see each route on a map with its speed graph, and export GPX. Without a recording, SpeedoME runs only while it's open.

**For GPS nerds.** Sky plot by constellation, C/N0 per satellite, raw NMEA, accuracy, fix age and filter rejects.

**Private.** No account, ads, analytics or Google Play Services. Your trips never leave your phone.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0): github.com/ShaptakNaskar/SpeedoME

**Category:** Maps & Navigation. **Tags:** speedometer, GPS, trip computer, satellites.
