# SpeedoME privacy policy

_Last updated: 26 September 2026_

SpeedoME is a GPS speedometer and trip computer. It is built to keep your data on your phone.

## What SpeedoME collects

- **Location** from your phone's GPS receiver, while the app is open or a trip is recording. It is used to show your speed, distance and route. The app can also read satellite status and, on the Nerd page, raw NMEA sentences.
- **Step counts** from the phone's step sensor, only in Walk & run mode and only with the Physical activity permission.
- **Compass and motion sensors**, only while the Nerd page or the heading / G-force readouts are on screen.
- **Your settings**, for example theme, units and targets.

## Where it goes

- **Trips you record** are stored on your phone, in the app's private database. They are never uploaded. You can delete them at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes them all.
- **Map tiles.** When the Map theme or a trip's map is on screen, the app downloads map tiles from [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org). Like any web request, this reveals your IP address and the map area being viewed to that server. OpenFreeMap's own privacy policy applies. Tiles are cached on your phone. Nothing else is sent with these requests: no identifier, account or trip data.
- **GPX export.** Only when you choose to export a trip does the file go where you send it (another app, a file or a share target).

SpeedoME has no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash reporting, and it does not use Google Play Services.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Precise location | Speed and distance come from GPS. |
| Location "Allow all the time" (optional) | Lets a trip restart itself if Android closes the app during it. |
| Notifications | The ongoing notification shows live speed and trip controls while a trip records. |
| Vibration | Buzzes at your speed limit, if you set one. |
| Physical activity (optional) | Step counting in Walk & run mode. |
| Ignore battery optimisations (optional) | Keeps GPS running reliably with the screen off. |
| Internet | Map tiles for the Map theme and trip maps. |

You can turn off any optional permission in Android's settings. The app keeps working without it.
